## This form: QS-F-049 — "Technical/Quality Review and Disposition Statement" (1 page)

### Header
- `ddin`: "RegenMed DDIN #". `cross_reference`: "Cross-Reference #".

### `items`: "Technical and Quality Review Elements", items 1 to 10
Always return **exactly 10 items**, numbers 1–10, even if the cells are blank.
Each item has two cells in the "Reviewed By/Date" columns on the right:
- `technical` (SignOff): the "Technical" column.
- `quality` (SignOff): the "Quality" column.
Each cell normally holds initials on top and a date below it (e.g. `NM` / `11-27-24`). Transcribe the date exactly as written, keeping its separators. If the cell says N/A, put `"N/A"` in `initials`. Only use what is written **inside** that item's Technical or Quality cell. Notes written elsewhere in the row's description text do not count.

### Item 10: `inc_number` and `inc_status`
Item 10 text contains "INC #: ____" and "Status: ____". Transcribe what is written after each label. If Status was corrected (e.g. "Open" crossed out, "Closed" written), use the final value. If nothing is written after the label, it is `""`.

Ignore the Disposition section at the bottom (signatures and release boxes). It isn't needed.
